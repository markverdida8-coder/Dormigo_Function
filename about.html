About DORMIGO

Bridging the gap between university students looking for secure, affordable boarding houses and property owners seeking reliable student tenants.

Our Mission

To eliminate the stress of manual room hunting for college students by delivering an intuitive, transparent digital platform that emphasizes safety, affordability, and genuine community living.

Our Vision

To become the premier trusted student accommodation ecosystem across university cities in the Philippines, empowering students with peace of mind and supporting local landlords with modern property tools.

Why DORMIGO is Different

Purpose-Built for Student Boarding Houses

Unlike hotel and travel booking apps designed for tourists, Dormigo is purpose-crafted for the student lifecycle. We know students care about curfew hours, internet reliability during finals, study areas, water refilling, and month-to-month contracts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a room?

Browse our Explore page, pick a boarding house with available rooms, select the room that fits your budget, and submit your move-in date and duration. The landlord receives your request instantly.

Are boarding houses verified?

Yes! Our Admin team verifies student IDs and landlord credentials to maintain safety and prevent fraudulent listings across all campus hubs.

How does payment work?

Once a landlord approves your booking, you can pay via GCash, bank transfer, or cash upon move-in, and record your transaction reference directly on the Payments page.
