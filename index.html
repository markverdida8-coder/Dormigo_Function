HOW DORMIGO WORKS

Finding student housing shouldn't be stressful. Here is how Dormigo guides you from search to move-in.

1

Search

Filter boarding houses by price, distance to campus, solo/shared room types, and amenities.

2

Compare

Inspect real photos, curated room rules, curfew policies, and honest peer reviews from students.

3

Request a Room

Pick an available room, specify your move-in date and duration, and send a direct note to the landlord.

4

Wait for Approval

Get instant notifications once the landlord reviews your application and approves your request.

5

Move In

Settle your payment, confirm your check-in details, and settle comfortably into your new home!
